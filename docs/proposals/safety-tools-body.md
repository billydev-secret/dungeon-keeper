# Safety Tools

Ways to protect yourself here, and how to use them. None of these is rude, and you never have to explain why you used one.

## Between you and another member

**Block them.** Discord's own block, from their profile. It stops their DMs and hides their messages from you across the whole of Discord, not just this server. They aren't told.

**`/nocontact add`** — closes every route the bot could use to put the two of you together: DMs, pairings, games, matching, voice rooms. It is silent, and it works in the places blocking doesn't reach. If a feature would have paired you, it quietly does something else instead — the other person is never shown a refusal with your name on it.

**A voice-room block.** If you host a room, you can block someone from joining it. That block is yours and stays yours.

You do not have to choose one. Blocking and `/nocontact` do different jobs and work fine together.

## Your DMs

**DM permissions** live in <#1469766598800838736>: **Closed**, **Ask**, or **Open**, changed whenever you like, as often as you like. Rule 5 here is that DMs and friend requests are opt-in, so Closed is a normal setting and not a statement.

## Stepping back from a feature

You can leave one thing without leaving everything, and none of these needs anyone's permission:

- **`/whisper forget-me`** — clears the whispers held about you. **`/whisper optout`** stops new ones.
- **`/guess optout`** — takes you out of Guess rounds.
- **`/bank shop`** — the opt-out controls for personal-role perks.
- **Wellness** — its own opt-out, inside the feature.

## When you need a person

**Open a ticket in <#1469781592854630580>** for anything else: someone who is bothering you, a copy of everything held about you, a full erasure, or a correction to something that's wrong. It's private, it's logged, and it goes straight to the mod team. Screenshots help. You never have to raise any of it in public chat.

Asking for your data or an erasure is not a complaint, and you don't have to give a reason for either.

## What's kept, and for how long

That's a separate question with its own doc — see **Privacy & Data Retention**. The short version: some of it goes on a clock, most of it lasts as long as the server does, and a no-contact order protecting someone else is deliberately kept even if they erase everything of their own.
